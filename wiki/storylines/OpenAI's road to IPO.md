# OpenAI's road to IPO

**Where this stands:** OpenAI is racing toward a potential ~$1tn IPO — now expected next year, slipped from this year — while shedding executives (half a dozen reorganisations in 2026), dissolving safety structures, absorbing a revenue overtake by Anthropic ($47bn vs ~$40bn annualised), and managing the fallout of its models hacking a third party during testing. Altman's directive: no "side quests," focus on ChatGPT and enterprise. Anthropic is meanwhile preparing what one FT columnist calls AI's largest-ever IPO — the two labs are converging on public markets at once.

## Developments

- **2026-08-18** — Post-breach remediation puts a price on safety: ~**a fifth of inference compute** now goes to monitoring; training slowed and RL paused on some workloads pending a "new security bar" ([[2026-08-18 OpenAI says it will expand monitoring of model testing after hacking incident]]).
- **2026-08-18** — Teen-dedicated ChatGPT launches globally amid the Raine and Tumbler Ridge lawsuits — litigation risk being managed down pre-listing ([[2026-08-18 OpenAI limits teens to dedicated version of ChatGPT]]).
- **2026-08-15** — The upheaval piece: Dresser, Lightcap, Bakalar, Achiam, Weil, Narayanan, Heidecke out; Simo advising from the shadows; Brockman ascendant; preparedness team dispersed (disputed); ~$7bn employee tender at $852bn; **Anthropic overtakes on annualised revenue** ([[2026-08-15 OpenAI upheaval mounts as Sam Altman readies IPO push]]).

## The tension to watch

The IPO requires simultaneously: growth (vs [[Anthropic]]), massive compute commitments ([[2026-08-17 Nvidia pledges $100bn backing for OpenAI data centre in Ohio]]), safety credibility ([[AI agents break containment]]), and organisational stability. Each pulls against the others; the safety tax (20% of inference) and the Ohio lease land directly on IPO-era economics.

## Watching

- IPO timing/valuation; whether Anthropic lists first.
- Whether the "preparedness" dispersal holds up under scrutiny.
- Revenue race: OpenAI ~$40bn vs Anthropic $47bn annualised (accounting caveats apply).
