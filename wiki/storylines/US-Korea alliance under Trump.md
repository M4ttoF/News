# US-Korea alliance under Trump

**Where this stands:** Hours before the annual Ulchi Freedom Shield exercises began on August 17, [[Donald Trump]] ordered the Pentagon to "substantially reduce" them, citing his relationship with Kim Jong Un and Seoul's refusal to back US action against Iran. The drills started on schedule with no one saying what gets cut. Seoul's security establishment now openly questions US reliability, and the pressure is toward South Korea taking wartime operational control of its own forces early.

## Developments

- **2026-08-17** — Trump orders the drills "substantially reduced" via Truth Social, calling them "inappropriate and hostile" toward a North Korea that is "unthreatening and respectful," days after Pyongyang called them "a rehearsal for a war of aggression." He ties the decision partly to Seoul declining to join US "denuclearization" efforts against Iran ([[2026-08-17 Trump orders Pentagon to scale back South Korea drills]]).
- **2026-08-17** — The analysis: read variously as an olive branch to Kim (Victor Cha) ahead of possible talks — where Kim may not even want a meeting, given Russian and Chinese support, and Seoul risks becoming a bargaining chip; expected to accelerate President Lee Jae Myung's push for **Opcon transfer** (pledged by 2030, possibly next year) ([[2026-08-17 Seoul rattled by Trump's threat to drills]]).

## The strain, accumulated (all per [[2026-08-17 Seoul rattled by Trump's threat to drills]])

- March 2026: Thaad missile-defence components relocated from Korea to the Middle East.
- USS George Washington strike group leaving the Pacific — no US carrier in the Indo-Pacific "for the foreseeable future."
- Tomahawk deliveries to Japan seriously delayed; Iran war has depleted stockpiles.
- US frustration over Seoul's un-deployed **$350bn investment pledge** from the tariff deal.
- Seoul not fully briefed on US F-16s flying near China's air-defence zone.

## Watching

- Whether Japan draws the same conclusion — the strain generalises to all US Indo-Pacific allies ([[2026-08-17 Trump orders Pentagon to scale back South Korea drills]]).
