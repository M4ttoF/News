# OpenAI–Apple trade secrets fight

**Where this stands:** Apple is suing OpenAI for alleged theft of hardware trade secrets as OpenAI builds AI consumer devices with hundreds of ex-Apple employees. OpenAI has now moved to dismiss (Aug 5 filing), recasting the case as an anti-poaching manoeuvre dressed up as trade-secrets litigation and leaning on California's pro-mobility employment law. Apple's injunction request is pending, with a **hearing set for October** before Judge Edward Davila.

## Developments

- **2026-08-06** — OpenAI files a **motion to dismiss**, its first court filing: the suit is "baseless and pretextual," intended to "make up for [Apple's] shortcomings in the market for talent." Argues Apple identified no specific confidential information beyond ordinary interview background; that supplier information was publicly obtainable; that Apple itself **authorised** Chang Liu's file retrieval after walking him out; and that Apple encouraged staff to use personal iCloud accounts for work, then failed to manage access on departure. Hearing set for October ([[2026-08-06 OpenAI says Apple lawsuit aims to stop employees leaving]]).
- **2026-08-04** — OpenAI's blog rebuttal: denies possessing secrets; alleges Apple's lawyers emailed the wrong person, invented a General-Counsel meeting, and went silent five months before suing; says Apple staff themselves asked ex-employee Chang Liu for help locating the disputed information. Same day: Apple files for preliminary injunction + expedited discovery, wants depositions from Liu and hardware chief Tang Tan ([[2026-08-04 OpenAI hits back at Apple trade secrets fight]]).
- **2026-07 (per background in same source)** — Apple filed suit: claims stolen hardware designs, names Liu and Tan, cites 400+ former employees at OpenAI.

## Background

Former partners (ChatGPT-in-iOS integration) turned rivals: OpenAI bought Jony Ive's studio io and hired heavily from Apple; Apple switched to Google to rebuild Siri ([[2026-08-04 OpenAI hits back at Apple trade secrets fight]]).

## Note on the employee count

Apple's suit and early coverage cited **400+** former Apple employees at OpenAI ([[2026-08-04 OpenAI hits back at Apple trade secrets fight]]); OpenAI's own filing says "hundreds of Apple designers and engineers" have left since io Products' founding ([[2026-08-06 OpenAI says Apple lawsuit aims to stop employees leaving]]). Not a contradiction, but the precise figure is contested framing rather than an established fact.

## Watching

- Injunction ruling (hearing October, Judge Edward Davila) and whether depositions of Tang Tan and Chang Liu are ordered.
- Whether OpenAI's device plans surface publicly during discovery.
- Whether the motion to dismiss narrows the case to the two named individuals or knocks it out entirely.
