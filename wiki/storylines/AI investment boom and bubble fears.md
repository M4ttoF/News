# AI investment boom and bubble fears

**Where this stands:** AI capital deployment continues at record scale — Amazon completed a $50bn OpenAI stake, Nvidia convened six asset managers for **$500bn+** in compute financing and then (Aug 17) directly backstopped **$105bn** of an OpenAI data centre — while the warnings have escalated from fund managers and Lex to a **central bank**: ECB economists now call a US tech correction *likely*, argue it can happen even if AI succeeds and valuations stay rational, and frame it as a euro-area financial-stability question. The boom and the bubble-warnings are accelerating simultaneously, and the funding is migrating from public markets to private credit — see [[AI infrastructure financing]].

## Developments

- **2026-08-18/19** — **The rates channel goes live.** Chip stocks lead a selloff (SOX -5.6%) as 30-year Treasuries hit 5.34% (highest since 2007) on Iran-war oil and fiscal fear — with Barclays naming **"AI-related corporate issuance"** as one of three causes of the long-end selloff ([[2026-08-18 US chip stocks slide as government borrowing costs hit multiyear highs]]). The mechanism piece: hyperscalers have pushed US IG issuance past $1.5tn YTD at 16.5-year average tenors — "are hyperscaler bonds the new Treasuries?" ([[2026-08-18 AI like a debt machine]]).
- **2026-08-19** — Froth exhibits: Unitree debuts +460% in Shanghai at 150x+ sales ([[2026-08-19 Humanoid robots don't deserve their superhuman valuations]]); Higgsfield 4x's its valuation in 8 months on a $20mn→$700mn revenue year ([[2026-08-17 Higgsfield valued at $5.4bn as Goldman and Intel back AI video startup]]); the AI super-rich reshape trophy-asset markets ([[2026-08-20 The new AI super-rich are reshaping the market for trophy assets]]).
- **2026-08-19** — Jane Street's **$15bn July loss** (Situational Awareness stake + AI stocks) vs Hudson River's record $11.4bn quarter — AI volatility as both wound and windfall ([[2026-08-19 Hudson River posts $11.4bn trading windfall]]).
- **2026-08-17** — **The ECB joins the warning side.** Its economists' blog post argues a US tech correction is likely *even in the rational scenario*: once a breakthrough technology succeeds and diffuses, risk shifts from individual firms (diversifiable, option-value upside) to the whole economy (undiversifiable, demands a higher risk premium) — so success itself de-rates valuations. Euro-area households hold ~€440bn of US tech equity; "a US AI fallout would not remain a US problem" ([[2026-08-17 US tech stock correction likely, warn ECB economists]]).
- **2026-08-17** — Same day, the deployment side accelerates again: [[Nvidia]] backstops **$105bn** for OpenAI's 8GW Ohio campus and Huang has to publicly deny it amounts to "circular financing" ([[2026-08-17 Nvidia pledges $100bn backing for OpenAI data centre in Ohio]]).
- **2026-08-11** — [[Nvidia]] forms a **$500bn+** financing partnership with Apollo, Blackstone, BlackRock, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs and KKR, backstopping up to 25% of project residual value. Justification: hyperscaler/neocloud spending "may reach over $8tn in the coming years," more than public markets can absorb ([[2026-08-11 Nvidia becomes the bank of AI]]).
- **2026-08-03** — Private money keeps flooding in despite the public sell-off: Olix triples to $3.3bn; recent rounds for Fractile, Etched ($10.3bn), SambaNova ($11bn) ([[2026-08-03 Olix triples valuation to 3.3bn]]). See [[Nvidia challengers - the AI chip startup wave]].
- **2026-08-03** — [[Palantir]] shares -25% YTD on AI-disruption fears before its earnings beat sent them +12% after hours — the volatility cuts both ways ([[2026-08-03 Palantir forecasts greater US demand for AI software]]).
- **2026-08-01** — Lex post-mortem on [[Situational Awareness]]: leveraged AI bets + market rout → banks called loans → fire-sale of public holdings to Citadel. Warns data-centre builders (~$9tn by 2030, debt-financed) of the same "temporal mismatch" (Man Group's phrase): chips may wear out before loans are repaid; model efficiency gains or cheap Chinese models (Kimi K3) could strand capacity ([[2026-08-01 Situational Awareness got the future right]]).
- **2026-07-31** — [[Amazon]] completed its $50bn OpenAI investment (~5% of $852bn) ahead of an expected 2027 IPO, waiving its own milestone conditions to do so ([[2026-07-31 Amazon completes 50bn investment in OpenAI]]).
