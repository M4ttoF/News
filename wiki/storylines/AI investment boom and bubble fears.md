# AI investment boom and bubble fears

**Where this stands:** AI capital deployment continues at record scale — Amazon just completed a $50bn OpenAI stake, private chip startups raise at tripling valuations — while public markets wobble: an AI-stock rout in July 2026 forced the leveraged Situational Awareness fund into a fire-sale to Citadel, and Lex projects data-centre builders will invest $9tn by 2030 on heavy debt. The boom and the bubble-warnings are accelerating simultaneously.

## Developments

- **2026-08-03** — Private money keeps flooding in despite the public sell-off: Olix triples to $3.3bn; recent rounds for Fractile, Etched ($10.3bn), SambaNova ($11bn) ([[2026-08-03 Olix triples valuation to 3.3bn]]). See [[Nvidia challengers - the AI chip startup wave]].
- **2026-08-03** — [[Palantir]] shares -25% YTD on AI-disruption fears before its earnings beat sent them +12% after hours — the volatility cuts both ways ([[2026-08-03 Palantir forecasts greater US demand for AI software]]).
- **2026-08-01** — Lex post-mortem on [[Situational Awareness]]: leveraged AI bets + market rout → banks called loans → fire-sale of public holdings to Citadel. Warns data-centre builders (~$9tn by 2030, debt-financed) of the same "temporal mismatch" (Man Group's phrase): chips may wear out before loans are repaid; model efficiency gains or cheap Chinese models (Kimi K3) could strand capacity ([[2026-08-01 Situational Awareness got the future right]]).
- **2026-07-31** — [[Amazon]] completed its $50bn OpenAI investment (~5% of $852bn) ahead of an expected 2027 IPO, waiving its own milestone conditions to do so ([[2026-07-31 Amazon completes 50bn investment in OpenAI]]).
