# Google's AI reorganisation

**Where this stands:** What was announced on August 5 as a leadership change is, on fuller reporting, a relocation of power: Google is moving control of its AI effort **from London back to Silicon Valley**, and the beneficiary is co-founder **Sergey Brin**, who holds no formal executive role but now sits beside the man running Gemini. [[Demis Hassabis]] retains the grander titles — Alphabet chief scientist, DeepMind chair — while [[Koray Kavukcuoglu]] takes day-to-day control as an SVP rather than a CEO. The stated reason is commercial urgency: Google's executives and board are worried about losing coding tools and enterprise AI to [[OpenAI]] and [[Anthropic]]. Alphabet shares fell 5%, four senior AI figures left, and staff at the London lab are reportedly taking recruiter calls.

## Developments

- **2026-08-06** — Fuller reporting from a dozen people familiar with the company: the reorganisation **shifts AI control from London to California**, cementing Brin's return to influence. Kavukcuoglu relocated to Mountain View last year and leads Gemini **from a desk beside Brin**. Framed as commercial urgency eclipsing DeepMind's research-led culture at the **$4.4tn** company. The transition had been planned for months ([[2026-08-06 Google's AI shake-up boosts Brin as Hassabis steps aside]]).
- **2026-08-06** — Analysis: the SVP-not-CEO title implies less autonomy and closer alignment with other divisions. Pichai conceded "areas we need to improve." Gemini, which a year ago had caught up, is **again perceived to have fallen behind**, specifically in AI coding agents ([[2026-08-06 Google seeks a sharper focus in AI after Hassabis move]]).
- **2026-08-05** — The announcement itself: Hassabis to Alphabet chief scientist and DeepMind chair; Kavukcuoglu to SVP; Jeff Dean departing; stock -5% ([[2026-08-05 Google DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis steps aside]]).

## Why Hassabis stepped back — contested

Two accounts sit in the record and are not reconciled:

- **Commercial frustration.** Several people familiar with Google's thinking say senior executives were frustrated by his lesser focus on commercial demands, and that making **AlphaFold freely available** caused internal tension because it produced little commercial return relative to its cost ([[2026-08-06 Google's AI shake-up boosts Brin as Hassabis steps aside]]).
- **Researcher's choice.** A person close to the company **disputes** any tension, noting Hassabis led the initial Gemini launch. A friend calls it a good outcome: he keeps influence **without relocating to the US** and gets more time for **Isomorphic Labs**. Yann LeCun frames it as a researcher preferring to do research over managing others ([[2026-08-06 Google's AI shake-up boosts Brin as Hassabis steps aside]]).

Both may be true. The AlphaFold detail is the load-bearing one: a Nobel-winning open release recorded internally as a commercial disappointment says something about the institution regardless of who left and why.

## Talent consequences

- **Jeff Dean**, 27-year veteran, leaves to found **Discovery Loop** with three colleagues — with Google backing ([[2026-08-06 Google's AI shake-up boosts Brin as Hassabis steps aside]]).

- The London lab reportedly in shock, with a former DeepMind executive at a rival saying multiple staff have already called about leaving ([[2026-08-06 Google's AI shake-up boosts Brin as Hassabis steps aside]]).
