# US-China chip war

**Where this stands:** The control regime has partially inverted. Washington now licenses H200 sales to China (up to 100,000 per company); it is **Beijing's NDRC** that rations what enters the mainland, protecting Huawei's domestic market while grudgingly admitting Nvidia chips for frontier training. Chinese labs (Moonshot K3, Alibaba, DeepSeek, Z.ai) have nearly closed the model gap; export controls remain the "own goal" that organised China's self-sufficiency drive.

## Developments

- **2026-08-19** — Beijing lets ByteDance and Tencent import ~10,000 H200s each; ~500k Nvidia H200s sit in inventory awaiting Chinese approvals; licensed chips parked in Hong Kong lack power capacity to run. Domestic chips for inference, Nvidia for training ([[2026-08-19 China eases limits on Nvidia H200 chips as AI race escalates]]).
- **2026-08-15** — The Manus unwind completes the picture of China's outbound controls: forced buyback from Meta at $2bn, Tencent becomes top shareholder, agentic AI weighed for the export-control list ([[2026-08-15 China poised to lift travel ban on Manus founders]]).
- **2026-08-16** — The smuggling channel: Malaysia under US pressure as the alleged backdoor for controlled chips ($390mn Singapore fraud case; Nvidia tightening due diligence) ([[2026-08-16 Malaysia profits from data centre boom]]).

## Watching

- Agentic AI landing on China's export-control list.
