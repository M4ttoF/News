# Private credit stress

**Where this stands:** The $2tn private credit industry is in its worst stretch since 2017: non-accrual loans at the biggest listed BDCs jumped to a median 2.8% of cost in Q2 (from 2.0%), Fitch counted record defaults in July, and the biggest listed funds are shrinking, waiving fees, or exploring wind-downs. Management is split between "we're in a credit cycle, denial is over" (Golub) and "issues are isolated" (Blue Owl, Ares). The epicentre is 2020–21 zero-rate vintage LBO debt. This matters beyond its own borders: private credit is the lender base for the AI build-out.

## Developments

- **2026-08-18** — The FT stress tally: BDC non-accruals at 2017 levels; record July defaults (Fitch); FS KKR at 7.1% troubled; KKR/BlackRock listed funds -15%+ over a year; BlackRock's TCPC selling loans and exploring wind-down; named blow-ups Medallia (Blackstone marks <50¢; Thoma Bravo abandoned a $5bn equity cheque), Cornerstone OnDemand, Affordable Care. Executives blame media coverage for outflows; Oppenheimer says bottom-quartile BDCs underperformed Treasuries over five years ([[2026-08-18 Private credit under strain as troubled loans swell]]).
- **2026-08-12** — The funding-cost channel: a Fed note (Haque/Wang, Y-14Q data) finds banks charge BDCs sharply more during hiking cycles without any deterioration in their own credit assessments — monopsony pricing from a market where 3 banks hold half the funding and BDCs never switch. Alphaville notes bond markets turned on BDCs simultaneously (spreads above SpaceX/Oracle), suggesting markets may be pricing default *correlation* in concentrated software-heavy books ([[2026-08-12 Business development companies are paying more to borrow]]).

## Why it's on the AI beat

- Private credit is where the AI build-out borrows once it outgrows bond markets — the Nvidia consortium's $500bn platforms, the Beignet/Sopaipilla lease structures ([[AI infrastructure financing]]).
- BDC books are software-heavy, and software revenue durability is uncertain "as corporate spending shifts to AI" ([[2026-08-18 Private credit under strain as troubled loans swell]]) — AI is simultaneously the sector's growth story and a disruption risk to its existing collateral.

## Watching

- Whether non-accruals keep climbing in Q3; whether "isolated issues" language survives another quarter.
- Contagion channel: whether stressed BDCs/credit funds pull back from data-centre lending just as the AI complex's borrowing needs peak ([[AI investment boom and bubble fears]]).
- The Medallia mark (Blackstone, <50¢) as a bellwether for 2020–21 software vintage.
