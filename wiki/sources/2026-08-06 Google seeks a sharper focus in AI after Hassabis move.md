# Google seeks a sharper focus in AI after Hassabis move

Richard Waters, analysis. Reads the reorganisation as two things at once: Google admitting its breadth in AI has become a focus problem, and a signal that **the competitive frontier is moving from frontier models to agentic systems**.

**On Google's problem**
- Google has "irons in more AI fires than any other big tech company" — its own chips, cloud platform, frontier models, search. That breadth is now the weakness: it exposes the need for sharper strategic focus.
- The title change is the tell: Hassabis had **CEO**; Kavukcuoglu is a **senior vice-president** — implying less autonomy and closer alignment with other Google divisions.
- Pichai conceded there are "areas we need to improve."
- Timeline of the whiplash: little more than **a year** since Google first fielded a frontier model that could hold its own against OpenAI and Anthropic (prompting a share-price rally) — and Gemini is **now perceived to have fallen behind again**, having lost ground specifically in the booming market for **AI coding agents**.
- The 2023 DeepMind/Google Brain merger, made when Google was reeling from ChatGPT, "looks ready for refinement" three years on.

**On the shifting frontier — the more important argument**
- Frontier models have been the centre of competition, but that is changing: **the gap between leaders is narrowing** and **stronger competition is emerging from Chinese models**.
- Attention is shifting to **agent-based systems** with the potential to transform research and knowledge work. For the departing Google executives, this looked like too good a moment to miss.
- The competitive edge increasingly lies in the software **"harnesses"** that control how agents call on and apply underlying models, plus the extra domain data used to boost their knowledge.
- Examples of the agentic-science wave: **Discovery Loop** (Jeff Dean and three colleagues) aims to transform scientific research by **automating the basic scientific process**; [[Anthropic]] announced a comparable initiative, **Claude Science**, in June; Hassabis is "leaning in" further to **Isomorphic Labs**.
- Other domains already in play: coding, scientific research, and generic white-collar work (via Claude CoWork). Musk said this week that **Grok is being trained on SpaceX's entire corpus of engineering data** to make it the best engineering AI.
- On AGI: Hassabis said he wants to spend more time on it and claims it is "close at hand." Waters notes there is **little agreement on what AGI would even entail**, while allowing that leading models are opening real new research vistas.
