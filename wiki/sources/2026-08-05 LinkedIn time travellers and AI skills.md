# Time travellers are using LinkedIn to teach us about artificial intelligence

FT Alphaville (Louis Ashworth) on the NBER paper "Time Travel on Professional Profiles" (Bloom, Moore, Simon, Wilkie-Rogers): at least 1 in 5 LinkedIn users retroactively edits descriptions of jobs they've already left. Because the underlying job is fixed, the edits reveal what skills workers *believe* the market now demands.

**Key points**
- 2020–2026 pattern: retroactive edits play up AI-tool use in past roles and de-emphasize DEI ("robots up, woke down").
- "Time-travel" edits closely precede employer changes — they're resume polishing for job searches.
- Most-represented editing group: AI product managers; least: dentists.
- AI is increasingly used to do the editing itself; usage declines with education level, except MBAs (driven by low-ranked programs).
