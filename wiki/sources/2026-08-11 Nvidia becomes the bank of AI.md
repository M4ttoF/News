# Nvidia becomes the bank of AI

FT *Due Diligence* newsletter (Julia Rock). [[Nvidia]] has formed a financing partnership with six of the largest private-capital and asset-management firms to raise **more than $500bn** in "compute financing platforms" — described as arguably the most ambitious financing plan ever to hit Wall Street.

**Key facts**
- Partners: **Apollo, Blackstone, BlackRock, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs, KKR**. Deal still subject to final agreement.
- The capital pools will fund spending across Nvidia's "ecosystem, including leading frontier AI labs, enterprises and AI clouds" at "attractive rates for Nvidia customers" — i.e. Nvidia is orchestrating the debt that buys its own chips.
- Jensen Huang said on X that Nvidia may provide a **"residual-value support mechanism" for up to 25%** of the value of projects its partners finance. Those guarantees are what unlock insurance/pension money seeking slightly-above-investment-grade returns.
- Rationale: public markets alone cannot absorb hyperscaler/neocloud spending that "may reach over $8tn in the coming years."
- Context: follows a $35bn Apollo/Blackstone AI financing the FT covered the prior week; DD had predicted such deals "may grow larger yet."
- Framing: former leveraged-buyout shops have repositioned as "regular-way financiers" to AI, energy and infrastructure.
