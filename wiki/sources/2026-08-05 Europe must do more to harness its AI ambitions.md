# Europe must do more to harness its AI ambitions

Op-ed by Marietje Schaake (Stanford HAI, author of *The Tech Coup*) ahead of an autumn European Council discussion on AI convened by council president António Costa. Argues the EU has no collective political course on AI while the US and China treat it as an instrument of state power, leaving Europe dependent on foreign platforms in ways that compromise national security.

**Key points**
- Claims the Trump administration treats AI as a source of hegemonic ambition; reports suggest [[Anthropic]]'s latest Mythos model was used by US intelligence agencies while European allies' access was limited.
- Cites a NATO summit in Ankara where European leaders confronted "an unreliable and possibly adversarial America".
- Prescriptions: EU-level crisis-style task forces, national AI security institutes, AI talent programmes, investment in sovereign compute, resilience at AI chokepoints.
- Notes bottom-up momentum already: universities decoupling from non-EU tech providers, intelligence agencies choosing domestic systems.
