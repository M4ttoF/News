# Stripe to buy start-up OpenRouter in $8bn deal

Stripe ($159bn valuation) buys **OpenRouter** — the marketplace for routing requests between AI models — for **$8bn** cash-and-stock, its largest acquisition, founded by OpenSea's Alex Atallah (investors: Alphabet, Sequoia, a16z). Collison's framing: "Tokens are the central currency for companies building with AI" — Stripe wants to own the metering/routing/payments layer of the AI economy: intelligent request routing, token-based "streaming payments" (via its Metronome buy), fraud defence, and **agentic commerce** (AI agents paying on customers' behalf).

Also disclosed: Stripe has launched a **joint bid with Advent for PayPal at ~$53bn** — a genuinely transformative consolidation attempt, arguably bigger news than the OpenRouter deal it's buried under.

Feeds [[AI investment boom and bubble fears]] (the picks-and-shovels M&A layer). The agentic-commerce push lands the same week as [[AI agents break containment]] — the industry is wiring agents into payments while the labs work out how to keep agents inside test sandboxes.
