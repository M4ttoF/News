# Donald Trump orders Pentagon to scale back military drills with South Korea

[[Donald Trump]] announced via Truth Social that he ordered defence secretary Pete Hegseth to **"substantially reduce"** the annual **Ulchi Freedom Shield** joint exercises with South Korea (Aug 17–27, too late to cancel outright), because the drills send a "hostile" signal to North Korea — a country he called "unthreatening and respectful" under his presidency — and citing his "very good relationship with Kim Jong Un."

Key facts:
- Trump explicitly linked the decision to Seoul's refusal to join US action on the **"Denuclearization of the Islamic Republic of Iran"** — "somewhat related." Seoul's presidential office said it would "make diplomatic efforts for US-Korea military co-operation" on the Strait of Hormuz situation.
- North Korea had on Friday called the exercises "a rehearsal for a war of aggression" — Trump's move follows Pyongyang's framing within days.
- Precedent: after the June 2018 Singapore summit with Kim, Trump cancelled exercises unilaterally, surprising his own advisers. Pyongyang has not responded to US outreach in recent years.
- Regional context stacking up: the **USS George Washington carrier strike group is leaving the Pacific for the Middle East** (no US carrier in the Indo-Pacific); the Pentagon warned allies of major weapons-delivery delays (Tomahawks to Japan) because the Iran war has depleted stockpiles; Trump officials are frustrated Seoul hasn't produced concrete projects for its promised **$350bn US investment** from the tariff deal.
- Sharpest quote: Mason Richey (Hankuk University) — "as crazy as it is irresponsible… Treating this any other way is normalising what is abnormal."
