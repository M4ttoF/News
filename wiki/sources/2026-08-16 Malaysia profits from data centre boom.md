# Malaysia profits from data centre boom

The quiet AI winner: Malaysia's Q2 GDP grew **6%** y/y (from 5.4%), powered by manufacturing +7.5% (chip-linked) and construction +6.6% (data centres). "The exemplar case of how to exploit opportunities from AI supply chain diversity" (Lowy Institute).

The position:
- **13% of the global back-end chip market** (packaging/assembly/testing — second only to China); world's sixth-biggest semiconductor exporter; chips = **40% of exports**. A "Silicon Valley of the East" lineage back to Intel in the 1970s.
- "China plus one" beneficiary: FDI **+41% in 2025 to ~$16bn**; July's US tariff round carved out semiconductors. Johor hosts **76 of Malaysia's 187** operational/planned data centres (sixth-biggest APAC market; TikTok, Nvidia, Microsoft on former palm-oil plantations; RM77bn into the Johor–Singapore special economic zone).
- The frictions: local protests over **energy and water consumption**; and Malaysia as the alleged **backdoor for smuggled US chips into China** — the $390mn Singapore fraud case, Nvidia tightening buyer due diligence, US pressure to crack down. "I can see the US losing patience" (Lowy).
