# Amazon completes $50bn investment in OpenAI

[[Amazon]] completed its full $50bn investment in [[OpenAI]] — ~5% of the $852bn startup — ahead of an expected 2027 IPO. The initial $15bn (Feb 2026) was to be followed by $35bn only if milestones (IPO or an AI breakthrough) were hit; Amazon invested it all anyway, per its Friday results.

**Key facts**
- The path opened when OpenAI renegotiated its Microsoft contract in April 2026, letting other clouds (AWS) serve OpenAI. Microsoft had earlier weighed legal action over the Amazon deal.
- Amazon plays both sides: up to $33bn committed to [[Anthropic]] ($18bn invested to date), using both labs to push its Trainium chips (vs Nvidia and Google's TPU) and to stock its cloud with frontier models.
- Frames OpenAI's continuing need for capital: training frontier models remains hugely costly and the startup is loss-making.
