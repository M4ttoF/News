# Google DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis steps aside in shake-up of AI lab

[[Demis Hassabis]] stepped down as CEO of [[Google DeepMind]]; he becomes DeepMind chair and chief scientist of Alphabet. Former lieutenant **Koray Kavukcuoglu** takes over as SVP of Google DeepMind — a title change suggesting DeepMind is being absorbed into Google's broader business. Google stock fell 5% on the announcement.

**Key facts**
- Chief scientist **Jeff Dean** is leaving to found his own startup, which Google will back.
- Context: despite DeepMind's pedigree (AlphaGo, Nobel-winning AlphaFold), Google has lagged younger labs [[OpenAI]] and [[Anthropic]], particularly in AI coding agents; it recently dismantled its Nobel-winning research team and seeded talent into Gemini-focused divisions.
- Google acquired DeepMind in 2014 for £400mn.
