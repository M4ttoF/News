# Google strikes $12bn AI chip deal with Marvell

Google expands its custom-TPU supply chain: a partnership with **Marvell** for TPU-ecosystem silicon (inference accelerators, storage/network/memory controllers, near-memory compute), paired with a warrant letting Google buy up to **58.9mn Marvell shares (~$12.2bn at $206.58)** — most tranches vesting **$500mn of co-developed product revenue per tranche** through 2033. Marvell +8% (now ~3x in 12 months); **Broadcom -5%** — Google's incumbent TPU partner (April deal running to 2031) just got a competitor.

Strategic context:
- Google is now **selling TPUs externally**, not just self-supplying — including a "mammoth financing operation" to supply **over $150bn of AI chips to [[Anthropic]]** (per earlier FT reporting; un-fetched). The equity-warrant structure echoes the vendor-financing pattern everywhere else in the build-out ([[AI infrastructure financing]]).
- Marvell is collecting strategic anchors: Nvidia invested $2bn in March (silicon photonics); it acquired Celestial AI (photonic chip-to-chip interconnect) — the same interconnect frontier as Olix ([[Nvidia challengers - the AI chip startup wave]]).
