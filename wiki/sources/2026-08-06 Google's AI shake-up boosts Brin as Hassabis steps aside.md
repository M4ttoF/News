# Google's AI shake-up boosts Brin as DeepMind's Hassabis steps aside

Reported from **a dozen people familiar** with the company, including current and former staff (additional reporting: Stephen Morris, George Hammond, Tim Bradshaw). This is the substantive follow-up to the bare announcement covered in [[2026-08-05 Google DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis steps aside]] — and it reframes the story.

**The actual shape of the reorganisation**
- Google is **shifting control of its AI effort from London back to Silicon Valley**, consolidating power in California.
- The change **cements the returning influence of co-founder Sergey Brin**, who had largely withdrawn from day-to-day operations for years and re-emerged as one of Google's most influential AI voices after ChatGPT launched. He holds **no formal executive position** but has sought to steer AI decision-making behind the scenes.
- **Koray Kavukcuoglu** — former DeepMind CTO — relocated from London to Mountain View last year and now leads Gemini development **from a desk beside Brin**.
- Characterised as a seismic shift at the **$4.4tn** company, in which "commercial urgency eclipses the research-led culture" that defined DeepMind for over a decade.
- The transition had been **planned for several months**, with Hassabis handing over operational responsibility for Gemini as Google accelerated model work.

**Why Hassabis stepped back — two accounts**
- Several people familiar with Google's thinking: senior executives had grown **frustrated by his lesser focus on the commercial demands** of the AI business. Specifically, his decision to make **AlphaFold freely available** became a source of internal tension — it generated little commercial return relative to the investment.
- **A person close to the company disputed** that there had been tensions, noting Hassabis led the initial Gemini launch and was invested in its success.
- **Yann LeCun** (ex-Meta chief AI scientist, who made a similar move in 2018) frames it as a researcher choosing to pursue a research vision directly rather than by managing others.
- **A friend of Hassabis**: a great outcome — he retains influence **without having to move to the US**, and is freed to spend more time on **Isomorphic Labs**, the AI drug-discovery company he founded inside Alphabet in 2021 and is "hugely passionate" about.
- Hassabis's own framing: stepping back to focus on the "big picture" and to influence the direction of AGI, which he says **"is close at hand."**

**Fallout inside the lab**
- Several current and former DeepMind employees say the reorganisation **sent shockwaves through the London lab**, with some fearing the end of the research culture Hassabis protected.
- One former DeepMind executive now at a rival says they have **already received calls from multiple staff ready to jump ship**.
- **Jeff Dean**, a 27-year Google veteran, is leaving to found **Discovery Loop** with three colleagues — **with Google backing**.
- **Alphabet shares fell 5%** after the announcements.

**Competitive context**
- Google is under pressure to show it can compete with [[OpenAI]] and [[Anthropic]] in **coding tools and enterprise AI**, where both have an early lead — a concern held by executives and board members.
- Counterweight: Google had a resurgence last year with **Gemini 3** and the viral success of its **Nano Banana** image-editing tool.
- Background: Pichai merged DeepMind with Google Brain in **2023**; the combined unit absorbed Google's remaining AI safety and research teams plus the Gemini apps business. Hassabis called it the **"AI engine room of Google."**
- Hassabis sold DeepMind to Google for **£400mn in 2014**; his Nobel Prize in Chemistry for AlphaFold came in **2024**.
- Pichai's framing: the company has to accelerate and stay focused on the AI frontier.
