# The new AI super-rich are reshaping the market for jets, yachts and cars

The full piece behind the FirstFT teaser: AI wealth (OpenAI, Anthropic, Nvidia employees and investors) is remaking the trophy-asset market. Global billionaires **+13% y/y to 3,302** (UBS); San Francisco median single-family home **$2.1mn in June, +25% y/y** (Compass — note: the brokerage, not the user's employer).

The colour: Flexjet's average client age dropped a decade; a data-centre builder charters jets while awaiting his Bombardier; clients want to pay in bitcoin, book by WhatsApp, skip "the theatre of traditional private aviation," and stock Fiji water instead of champagne. Lamborghini's 150-colour Urus wins the under-30s; Ferrari's electric Luce polarises traditionalists but lands in Silicon Valley; a twenties LA AI entrepreneur's Rolls-Royce Cullinan resprayed bright blue and orange. Nvidia employees buy "functional machines," fast long-range boats for the Pacific, heavily tinted.
