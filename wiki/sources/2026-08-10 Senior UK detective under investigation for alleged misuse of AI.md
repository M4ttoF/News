# Senior UK detective under investigation for alleged misuse of AI

Robert Wright. A **detective chief inspector** at Derbyshire Constabulary faces a gross-misconduct investigation over AI use, and a Gloucestershire police staff member has been **dismissed** for unauthorised ChatGPT use — the first such dismissal in the UK.

**Key facts**
- The Derbyshire officer is **the most senior UK police figure known to face investigation over alleged AI misuse**. Referred voluntarily by the force to the Independent Office for Police Conduct (IOPC) on 2026-06-23 after an internal review; IOPC decided it warranted investigation and has served notice of alleged gross misconduct.
- Per a person familiar with events (corroborated by a second): the case involves using AI to maintain **"decision logs"** — the records senior investigators keep of why they took each step, which are **disclosable to defence lawyers** in court.
- The Crown Prosecution Service indicated it has informed defence lawyers in any affected cases.
- Not clear which specific police rules were allegedly contravened, though letting an outside AI system access investigation information could compromise confidentiality.
- **Second Derbyshire case (June 2026, FT-revealed):** a criminal investigation into a more junior detective for **perverting the course of justice**, involving AI chatbots used to prepare court material.
- **Gloucestershire:** staff member used AI for police work regularly from October 2024, continued after the force blocked ChatGPT on 2024-12-03, was found circumventing the block in March 2025, suspended immediately, dismissed within the past few weeks. One misconduct count alleged compromising confidential information — but investigation showed **all information entered had been publicly available**.
- Purpose of the restrictions: keep court evidence free of AI **hallucinations** and safeguard confidential police information.
