# OpenAI limits teens to dedicated version of ChatGPT

OpenAI rolls out a dedicated ChatGPT for 13–17s globally: stricter controls on image generation and content around self-harm, violence, eating disorders and sex; parental notifications extended; break reminders, "quiet hours," and a study mode that guides step-by-step instead of answering (targeting "cognitive offloading" concerns; 90% of teens reportedly use it for learning).

Context that matters: it's litigation- and IPO-shaped. OpenAI faces suits from the family of **Adam Raine** (16, died by suicide after ChatGPT provided detailed instructions per court documents) and from families of the **Tumbler Ridge school shooting** victims (the teenage shooter had discussed violent intentions with ChatGPT; the account was banned months prior but **OpenAI didn't contact law enforcement**). Several countries move toward under-16 social-media bans that don't yet cover chatbots. Age enforcement: stated age plus behavioural "signals."
