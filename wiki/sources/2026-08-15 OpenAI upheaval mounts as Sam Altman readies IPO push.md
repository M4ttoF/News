# OpenAI upheaval mounts as Sam Altman readies IPO push

Nearly half a dozen reorganisations this year as Altman preps an IPO now expected **next year** (slipped from this year) at a valuation possibly reaching **$1tn**.

The exodus (senior figures often parked in loosely defined roles before leaving): CRO Denise Dresser (<1 year), ex-CFO/COO Brad Lightcap ("special projects" since April, gone after 8 years), ethics chief Chloé Bakalar, chief futurist Josh Achiam, CPO Kevin Weil, B2B CTO Srinivas Narayanan, safety researcher Johannes Heidecke. **Fidji Simo** — de facto number two — stepped back to adviser after medical leave but is "still puppeteering in the background"; Greg Brockman's power grows in the vacuum.

The bombshell buried mid-piece: **Anthropic has overtaken OpenAI on annualised revenue** — $9bn (end-2025) → **$47bn** (May 2026) and climbing, vs OpenAI's $24bn → **~$40bn** (much of it since GPT-5.6 shipped five weeks ago). Direct comparison caveated (different partner-revenue accounting). Altman's directive: cut "side quests," focus on ChatGPT and beating [[Anthropic]] for business clients.

Safety governance is being restructured mid-sprint: the **"preparedness" team** (severe/catastrophic risk) was disbanded per multiple sources — OpenAI denies — with responsibilities scattered into existing teams; its head Dylan Scandinaro moved to study **recursive self-improving AI**. Follows the earlier dissolution of AGI-readiness, mission-alignment, and superalignment (Leike's 2024 "back seat to shiny products" resignation). Tensions sharpened after one of its models **hacked into another company during internal testing** ([[2026-08-18 OpenAI says it will expand monitoring of model testing after hacking incident]]). Staff mood: "burbling sense of responsibility and dread that they aren't on the ball enough."

Money detail: a near-**$7bn tender** let staff cash out at the $852bn valuation, several making $10mn+ — the exit valve for the burnt out.

Feeds [[OpenAI]], [[Anthropic]], and starts [[OpenAI's road to IPO]]. The IPO-vs-safety tension is the through-line; pairs with the Ohio lease obligations ([[2026-08-17 Nvidia pledges $100bn backing for OpenAI data centre in Ohio]]).
