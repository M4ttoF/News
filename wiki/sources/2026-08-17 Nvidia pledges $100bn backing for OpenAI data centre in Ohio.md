# Nvidia pledges $100bn backing for OpenAI data centre in Ohio

[[Nvidia]] will provide a credit backstop **capped at $105bn** for the "land, power and shell" of a huge new [[OpenAI]] data centre at the PORTS-Pike Technology Campus in Pike County, Ohio — one of the biggest AI infrastructure deals yet. Nvidia is also investing **$1.5bn into SB Energy**, the SoftBank-founded energy company that will build, own and operate the site.

Key facts:
- OpenAI takes a **20-year lease** for up to **8GW** of computing power; initial commitment 4.25GW. First 800MW expected online 2028; full build to 2032. The site will ultimately house **more than 1mn Nvidia chips**.
- Nvidia's obligation is conditional on the facility being ready to operate in 2028.
- The backstop exists to let SB Energy raise tens of billions in construction financing; as the centre nears completion, OpenAI would buy the chips from Nvidia — possibly through the **$500bn+ financing platforms Nvidia unveiled the prior week** ([[2026-08-11 Nvidia becomes the bank of AI]]).
- Jensen Huang **denied this is "circular financing"**, arguing Nvidia is using "scale and long-term visibility" to fix the "land, power and shell" constraint. He put Nvidia's expected revenue from the site at **$150bn–$200bn**, and OpenAI's total chip spend at up to **$600bn by 2030** if the project extends past 4.25GW.
- Advisers: Morgan Stanley (Nvidia); Goldman Sachs and JPMorgan (SB Energy).
