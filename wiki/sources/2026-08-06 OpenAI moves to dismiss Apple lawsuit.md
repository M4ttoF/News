# OpenAI says Apple's trade secrets lawsuit aims to stop employees leaving

[[OpenAI]] filed to **dismiss** [[Apple]]'s trade-secrets suit in federal court in California (Wednesday Aug 5, reported Aug 6), calling the case "baseless and pretextual" — an attempt to stem an employee exodus and "make up for its shortcomings in the market for talent... and its failures to integrate AI into its products".

**Key facts**
- First formal legal rebuttal (the earlier blog post was PR — [[2026-08-04 OpenAI hits back at Apple trade secrets fight]]).
- Core arguments: Apple identifies no specific stolen confidential information; supplier info was legally/publicly obtainable; California law encourages employee mobility.
- On Chang Liu: file retrieval after resignation was "requested and authorised by Apple itself" after he was "hastily walked out of the door". OpenAI says Apple encouraged personal iCloud use for work, intermingling data.
- On Tang Tan: interview questions were normal employer background questions, not solicitation of secrets.
- Scale of exodus: "hundreds" of Apple designers/engineers joined OpenAI since the $6.4bn io Products acquisition; Apple has sent legal letters to dozens of them.
- OpenAI device: palm-sized, screenless, takes audio/visual cues from environment (per earlier FT reporting).
- Apple's injunction motion (filed Aug 3-4) has a hearing scheduled for **October**. Judge: Edward Davila (of Theranos-trial fame).
