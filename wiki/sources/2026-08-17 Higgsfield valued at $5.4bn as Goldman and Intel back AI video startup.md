# Higgsfield valued at $5.4bn as Goldman and Intel back AI video start-up

AI video platform Higgsfield (founded by ex-Snap exec Alex Mashrabov, launched 2025): **$400mn at $5.4bn** — 4x its $1.3bn valuation of eight months ago (DST, Goldman, Liberty Global, Intel, NTT DOCOMO Ventures et al).

The traction: **$700mn annualised revenue in August, up from $20mn a year earlier**; 30mn+ users in 238 countries; business customers went from <25% of revenue in January to the majority now. Use case shift: brands (e.g. Dollar Shave Club) running AI marketing systems producing **multiple videos a day** rather than campaign assets — displacing creative agencies. Goldman sizes the creator economy at $480bn by 2027. Raise partly earmarked for "substantial compute reservations" — even the app layer is hoarding compute.

Feeds [[AI investment boom and bubble fears]] (application-layer revenue is real and violent — a $20mn→$700mn year), [[AI and white-collar work]] (creative agencies as the displaced cohort).
