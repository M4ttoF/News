# James Dacombe, 25, triples AI chip start-up's valuation to $3.3bn

London-based AI chip startup **Olix** (founded 2024 by 25-year-old James Dacombe) raised $312mn led by Fundomo, with Arm, Hudson River Trading, and Reed Hastings — valuation $3.3bn, up from ~$1bn in February when it raised $220mn.

**Key facts**
- Thesis: the single-GPU-for-everything era is over; custom silicon for high-volume LLM inference wins on cost. Differentiator: photonic interconnect (lasers moving data between chip clusters) and avoiding scarce components (leading-edge silicon, HBM, advanced packaging).
- Tape-out later this year; first products to customers next year; 140+ staff (London, Bristol, Toronto, Austin); ex-Wise CFO Matt Briers joins. Used AI tools heavily to build its compiler — the proof point for this round.
- The Nvidia-challenger funding wave despite public-market jitters: Fractile $220mn (May), Etched $300mn at $10.3bn (July), SambaNova $1bn at $11bn (July).
- Dacombe also runs brain-monitoring startup CoMind (~$100mn raised).
