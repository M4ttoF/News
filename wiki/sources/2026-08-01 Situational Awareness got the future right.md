# Situational Awareness got the future right but misread the past

Lex column (John Foley) on the near-collapse of Leopold Aschenbrenner's [[Situational Awareness]] fund: leveraged AI-stock bets met a sharp downturn, banks called loans, and the fund sold most of its public securities to Citadel in a liquidity crunch.

**Key facts**
- Of the 29 US-listed stocks the fund held at end-March, the average share price fell 21% in the last month; Sandisk (a large holding) fell 45%.
- Aschenbrenner claims the fund is still up 80% this year; it retains unleveraged stocks and a stake in still-private [[Anthropic]].
- The cautionary parallel: AI data-centre builders on course to invest $9tn by 2030 (Lex estimate), heavily debt-financed — Man Group calls it a "temporal mismatch". Risks: chips wearing out before loans are repaid, model efficiencies making data centres superfluous, cheaper Chinese models (Kimi K3) gaining traction.
- Thesis: you can be directionally right about AI and still come undone on leverage, timing, and specific winners.
