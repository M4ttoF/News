# China poised to lift travel ban on Manus founders

Endgame of the Meta–Manus forced unwind: Beijing will soon lift the travel ban on Manus CEO Xiao Hong and chief scientist Ji Yichao (held in China since March over the investigation into [[Meta]]'s $2bn acquisition, which the NDRC blocked in April).

The unwind mechanics:
- Former investors (Tencent, ZhenFund, HSG) plus management **buy Manus back from Meta at the same ~$2bn valuation**. Benchmark and smaller US investors exit; **Tencent absorbs their shares and becomes largest (minority) shareholder**. Manus stays independent, Singapore-based; ARR expected to hold above **$300mn**; version 2.0 launches after the deal settles.
- The precedent Beijing set: using Singapore as a staging post for selling Chinese AI companies to US buyers doesn't work. Beijing is also considering **adding agentic AI to its export-control list** to close the loophole.
- The carrot with the stick: regulators told Manus its operations won't be affected once the unwind satisfies requirements — "tightening control… while also allowing companies and entrepreneurs to develop their businesses if they abide by the rules."

Feeds [[China's open-weight AI push]] (agentic AI as a controlled strategic asset), [[Meta]] (second forced retreat from a China-linked position), [[US-China chip war]]-adjacent. Note: Manus agents are exactly the technology class in [[AI agents break containment]] — Beijing is moving to export-control the thing the US labs are struggling to contain.
