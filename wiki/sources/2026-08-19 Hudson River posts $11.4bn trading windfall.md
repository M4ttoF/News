# Hudson River posts $11.4bn trading windfall as it seizes on market tumult

Hudson River Trading: record **$11.4bn quarterly trading revenue, $7.4bn net profit** (Q2), riding volatility from the Iran war and AI-stock swings. Prop-trading cohort (Jane Street, Hudson River, Citadel Securities) made $60bn+ in 2025 and is beating bank desks.

The fact that matters for our beat: **Jane Street reported a $15bn July loss after an investment in Aschenbrenner's Situational Awareness hedge fund and other AI stocks backfired** — though it still made $40bn+ net trading revenue in the year to last Friday. This finally explains part of the $35bn-loss ecosystem around [[Situational Awareness]]: the fund's collapse burned its own investors, Jane Street included.

Also: prop firms are now AI-capex players themselves — Hudson River's head of AI: "The amount of compute I'll have next year versus this year looks exponential."
