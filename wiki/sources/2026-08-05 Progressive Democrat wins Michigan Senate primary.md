# Progressive Democrat wins in Michigan in rebuke to party establishment

*Off-beat for this wiki (fetched before link scoping was fixed): US politics, not AI.* Progressive Abdul El-Sayed beat establishment-backed Haley Stevens 48.5%–47.5% in Michigan's Democratic Senate primary despite being outspent ~4:1 (including ~$31mn from an AIPAC-backed Super PAC for Stevens — its largest single-race spend ever). He faces Republican Mike Rogers in November in a must-win state for Democratic Senate hopes; polls had suggested Stevens was the stronger general-election candidate. Follows Mamdani-endorsed progressive sweeps in NYC congressional primaries earlier in the summer.
