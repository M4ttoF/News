# OpenAI hits back at Apple over 'oddly personal' trade secrets fight

[[OpenAI]] published a blog post rebutting [[Apple]]'s trade-secrets lawsuit, calling it "careless, aggressive and oddly personal" and denying it has or wants Apple trade secrets. Same day, Apple sought a preliminary injunction and expedited discovery/depositions.

**Key facts**
- Background: Apple sued last month (July 2026) claiming OpenAI stole hardware designs for its AI consumer devices; 400+ former Apple employees now at OpenAI. Tension traces to OpenAI's acquisition of Jony Ive's design studio io and the Apple–Google Siri partnership.
- Apple names ex-employee Chang Liu (allegedly accessed secrets after leaving in January) and OpenAI chief hardware officer Tang Tan (allegedly solicited proprietary info in interviews).
- OpenAI counterclaims: Apple's lawyers emailed the wrong person ("confusing two Asian last names"), falsely claimed a meeting with OpenAI's General Counsel, said issues were "resolving", then went silent five months before suing; Apple employees themselves asked Liu to help locate the information.
- OpenAI and Apple were previously partners on integrating AI into Apple products.
