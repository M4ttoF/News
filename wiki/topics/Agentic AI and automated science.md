# Agentic AI and automated science

Standing topic: the argument that the competitive frontier in AI is moving **from the models themselves to the systems built around them** — and that the first big application is automating scientific research.

## The thesis

As of August 2026 the frontier-model race is losing its status as the only game: the gap between leading models is narrowing and Chinese open models are competitive. Attention is shifting to **agent-based systems** that can transform research and knowledge work ([[2026-08-06 Google seeks a sharper focus in AI after Hassabis move]]).

Where the edge is said to live now:

- The software **"harnesses"** that control how agents call on and apply underlying frontier models.
- The **extra domain data** used to boost an agent's specialist knowledge.

If that's right, the advantage shifts away from whoever has the best raw model and toward whoever best wires a model into a specific domain — which is precisely why senior researchers are leaving large labs to found narrow companies.

## Who is doing it

- **Discovery Loop** — founded by **Jeff Dean** and three colleagues leaving Google, with Google backing. Aim: transform large areas of scientific research by **automating the basic scientific process** ([[2026-08-06 Google's AI shake-up boosts Brin as Hassabis steps aside]], [[2026-08-06 Google seeks a sharper focus in AI after Hassabis move]]).
- **Claude Science** — a comparable [[Anthropic]] initiative announced in June 2026 ([[2026-08-06 Google seeks a sharper focus in AI after Hassabis move]]).
- **Isomorphic Labs** — [[Demis Hassabis]]'s AI drug-discovery company, founded inside Alphabet in 2021; he is now "leaning in" further to it ([[2026-08-06 Google's AI shake-up boosts Brin as Hassabis steps aside]]).
- **Grok / SpaceX** — Musk said in August 2026 that Grok is being trained on **SpaceX's entire corpus of engineering data** to make it the best AI for engineering — the same domain-data logic applied outside science ([[2026-08-06 Google seeks a sharper focus in AI after Hassabis move]]).
- Other domains already being worked: coding, and generic white-collar work via Claude CoWork ([[2026-08-06 Google seeks a sharper focus in AI after Hassabis move]]).

## Evidence it's more than a pitch

The strongest concrete datapoint in this wiki is not a startup announcement but a result: Stanford's **Evo 2** designed 16 functional synthetic phage genomes that outperformed their natural template ([[2026-08-06 AI creates first synthetic viruses]]). That is generative design producing a working biological artefact — the kind of thing "automating the scientific process" has to mean if it means anything. See [[AI in the life sciences]].

## Watching

- Whether Discovery Loop or Claude Science ship anything demonstrable, or remain thesis-stage.
- Whether the "harness plus domain data" framing survives the next frontier-model release, or gets absorbed by it.
- The AGI question hovering over all of it: Hassabis says AGI "is close at hand," while there is little agreement on what it would even entail ([[2026-08-06 Google seeks a sharper focus in AI after Hassabis move]]).
