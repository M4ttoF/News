# EU AI Act

The EU's landmark AI law, in force since **August 2024**, rolling out in phases. The world's first comprehensive AI regulation; its implementation is the live test of whether AI can be regulated without strangling adoption.

## Rollout timeline (as known)

- **Aug 2024** — Act enters into force.
- **Aug 2, 2026** — First consumer-facing transparency obligations enforceable: companies must disclose AI interactions (chatbots) and label AI-generated images/audio/video ("deepfakes" defined broadly). Commission can begin fining. Also from this date: Commission powers to inspect general-purpose AI models posing "systemic risks" — fines up to 3% of global turnover or €15mn, whichever is higher ([[2026-08-01 EU AI labels cookie banner moment]]).
- **Dec 2027** — Requirements for "high-risk" systems, already postponed under industry pressure ([[2026-08-01 EU AI labels cookie banner moment]]).

## The debate

- Industry: guidance arrived weeks before deadlines, definitions are over-broad ("label stops meaning anything" — CCIA), compliance costs disproportionate (Eurocommerce, Zalando).
- Supporters: transparency is pro-consumer and even pro-business — Booking.com finds disclosed-AI service outcomes measurably better ([[2026-08-01 EU AI labels cookie banner moment]]).
- The recurring criticism is unpredictability rather than strictness ([[2026-08-01 EU AI labels cookie banner moment]]).
