# OpenAI

Maker of ChatGPT, led by Sam Altman. Valued at **$852bn**, loss-making, with a public listing expected in 2027 ([[2026-07-31 Amazon completes 50bn investment in OpenAI]]). Moving beyond models into consumer hardware — the root of its legal war with [[Apple]].

## Recent developments

- **2026-08-04** — Published a combative rebuttal to Apple's trade-secrets lawsuit; Apple sought a preliminary injunction the same day. See [[OpenAI–Apple trade secrets fight]] ([[2026-08-04 OpenAI hits back at Apple trade secrets fight]]).
- **2026-07-31** — [[Amazon]] completed its full $50bn investment (~5% stake), enabled by the April renegotiation of OpenAI's Microsoft contract that let AWS serve OpenAI ([[2026-07-31 Amazon completes 50bn investment in OpenAI]]).
- **2026-08-02** — Its tools are reshaping security research: ChatGPT found 50+ macOS bugs for startup Bynario; Apple credits OpenAI tools in its own security fixes ([[2026-08-02 Apple struggles with AI bug hunters]]).

## Background

- Acquired io, the design studio of ex-Apple design chief Jony Ive; 400+ former Apple employees now work there, per Apple's lawsuit. Chief hardware officer: Tang Tan (ex-Apple).
- Cited alongside [[Anthropic]] as the labs Google has lagged behind, especially in coding agents ([[2026-08-05 Google DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis steps aside]]).
