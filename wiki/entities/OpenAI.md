# OpenAI

Maker of ChatGPT, led by Sam Altman. Valued at **$852bn**, loss-making, targeting an IPO **next year at up to ~$1tn** (timing slipped from 2026; earlier reporting said 2027) ([[2026-08-15 OpenAI upheaval mounts as Sam Altman readies IPO push]]) — see [[OpenAI's road to IPO]]. Annualised revenue ~$40bn, **overtaken by [[Anthropic]]'s $47bn**. Latest model family GPT-5.6 (incl. **Sol**). Moving beyond models into consumer hardware — the root of its legal war with [[Apple]].

- **2026-08-02** — Its tools are reshaping security research: ChatGPT found 50+ macOS bugs for startup Bynario; Apple credits OpenAI tools in its own security fixes ([[2026-08-02 Apple struggles with AI bug hunters]]).

## Background

- Acquired io Products, the design studio of ex-Apple design chief Jony Ive, for **$6.4bn** in 2025; 400+ former Apple employees now work there, per Apple's lawsuit (OpenAI says "hundreds"). Chief hardware officer: Tang Tan (ex-Apple). Ive is not a defendant in the lawsuit ([[2026-08-06 OpenAI says Apple lawsuit aims to stop employees leaving]]).
- The device in development: **palm-sized, screenless**, taking audio and visual cues from the environment ([[2026-08-06 OpenAI says Apple lawsuit aims to stop employees leaving]]).
- Sam Altman promotes consumer uses that draw backlash — an August 2026 post suggesting ChatGPT generate a personalised morning podcast for the school-run was widely mocked ([[2026-08-07 Can AI improve my parenting]]).
- Cited alongside [[Anthropic]] as the labs Google has lagged behind, especially in coding agents ([[2026-08-05 Google DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis steps aside]]).
