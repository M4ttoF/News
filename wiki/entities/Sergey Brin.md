# Sergey Brin

Google co-founder. Having largely withdrawn from day-to-day operations for several years, he **re-emerged as one of the company's most influential voices on AI strategy after ChatGPT launched**, taking an increasingly active role in Gemini's development ([[2026-08-06 Google's AI shake-up boosts Brin as Hassabis steps aside]]).

The notable structural fact: he has **no formal executive position**, yet people familiar with the company say he has sought to steer AI decision-making behind the scenes ([[2026-08-06 Google's AI shake-up boosts Brin as Hassabis steps aside]]).

## Recent developments

- **2026-08-06** — The reorganisation of Google's AI leadership is reported as cementing his returning influence: control moves from London to Silicon Valley, and [[Koray Kavukcuoglu]] leads Gemini development **from a desk beside Brin** ([[2026-08-06 Google's AI shake-up boosts Brin as Hassabis steps aside]]). See [[Google's AI reorganisation]].

## Why it matters

The seating arrangement is the story. Formal authority over Google's AI now runs through an SVP rather than a CEO, while informal authority sits with a founder holding no title. That combination makes the reporting line harder to read from outside — and is worth tracking against [[Demis Hassabis]]'s grander-but-detached Alphabet chief scientist role.
