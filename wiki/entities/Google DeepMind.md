# Google DeepMind

Alphabet's AI lab (acquired 2014 for £400mn), home of AlphaGo and Nobel-winning AlphaFold. Now confirmed to be losing autonomy: control of Google's AI effort is moving **from London to Silicon Valley**, and the lab is aligned closer to Google's product divisions. See [[Google's AI reorganisation]].

## Recent developments

- **2026-08-06** — The reorganisation's real shape: AI control shifts from London to Mountain View, cementing the influence of returning co-founder [[Sergey Brin]]; [[Koray Kavukcuoglu]] runs Gemini development from a desk beside him. Reported as commercial urgency eclipsing the lab's research-led culture. Several current and former employees describe shockwaves through the London lab and fears the research culture is over; a former executive now at a rival says multiple staff have already called about leaving ([[2026-08-06 Google's AI shake-up boosts Brin as Hassabis steps aside]]).
- **2026-08-06** — Also holds a **$75mn research partnership with film studio A24** exploring AI in film-making ([[2026-08-10 Boehly's Eldridge rolls out AI across portfolio]]).

- **2026-08-05** — Founder-CEO [[Demis Hassabis]] stepped aside (→ DeepMind chair + Alphabet chief scientist). **Koray Kavukcuoglu** now leads as *SVP of Google DeepMind* — the demoted title reads as absorption into Google. Chief scientist **Jeff Dean** is leaving to found a Google-backed startup. Google stock fell 5% on the news ([[2026-08-05 Google DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis steps aside]]).

## Background

- Despite its research pedigree, Google has lagged [[OpenAI]] and [[Anthropic]], notably in AI coding agents; it recently dismantled the Nobel-winning research team and pushed talent toward Gemini divisions ([[2026-08-05 Google DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis steps aside]]).
- Formed in its current shape in **2023**, when Pichai merged DeepMind with Google Brain as Google reeled from ChatGPT; the combined unit absorbed Google's remaining AI safety and research teams plus the Gemini apps business. Hassabis called it the **"AI engine room of Google"** ([[2026-08-06 Google's AI shake-up boosts Brin as Hassabis steps aside]]).
- Making **AlphaFold freely available** reportedly became a source of internal tension — little commercial return for the investment — though a person close to the company disputes there were tensions at all ([[2026-08-06 Google's AI shake-up boosts Brin as Hassabis steps aside]]).
