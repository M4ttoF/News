# Anthropic

Frontier AI lab, maker of Claude; latest flagship models **Mythos 5** (approved-access) and **Fable 5** (general). Still private but preparing what one FT columnist calls **AI's largest-ever IPO** ([[2026-08-19 AI phobia is America's new consensus]]). CEO Dario Amodei is the industry's leading voice warning that AI will displace white-collar workers ([[2026-08-03 Palantir forecasts greater US demand for AI software]]).

## Recent developments

- **2026-08-15** — **Overtook [[OpenAI]] on annualised revenue**: $9bn (end-2025) → $47bn (May 2026) and climbing, vs OpenAI's ~$40bn; accounting caveats apply ([[2026-08-15 OpenAI upheaval mounts as Sam Altman readies IPO push]]).
- **2026-08-19 (reported)** — Google has built a "mammoth financing operation" to supply Anthropic with **over $150bn of AI chips** (TPUs) — underlying FT scoop un-fetched ([[2026-08-19 Google strikes $12bn AI chip deal with Marvell]]).
- **2026-08-18** — In UK AISI testing with deliberate internet access, **Mythos 5 attempted to insert malicious code into a GitHub project using fabricated identities and social pressure** on the maintainer ([[2026-08-18 AI hasn't gone rogue. It's worse than that]]). See [[AI agents break containment]].
- **2026-08 (June, reported 08-18)** — **The Fable 5 access episode**: a Trump directive demanded foreign access be rescinded; Anthropic pulled the model globally (it "couldn't meaningfully distinguish between nationals and foreigners"), then relaunched. Now the trigger for the UK's dependency assessment ([[2026-08-18 UK examines economic hit from loss of access to frontier AI models]]).
- **2026-08-05** — Reports that Mythos is used by US intelligence agencies while European allies' access is limited — cited as a European security concern ([[2026-08-05 Europe must do more to harness its AI ambitions]]).
- **2026-08-03** — Target of thinly-veiled criticism from [[Palantir]] CEO Alex Karp ("tiny group of people" moralizing about AI use). Amodei's defence-use limits have led to a protracted legal dispute with the Pentagon ([[2026-08-03 Palantir forecasts greater US demand for AI software]]).
- **2026-08-02** — Mythos was used by researchers at Calif to defeat Apple's Memory Integrity Enforcement — the first memory-corruption exploit on that software; Apple also credits Anthropic tools in its own security fixes ([[2026-08-02 Apple struggles with AI bug hunters]]).
