# Anthropic

Frontier AI lab, maker of Claude; latest flagship model **Mythos**. Still private; CEO Dario Amodei is the industry's leading voice warning that AI will displace white-collar workers ([[2026-08-03 Palantir forecasts greater US demand for AI software]]).

## Recent developments

- **2026-08-05** — Reports that Mythos is used by US intelligence agencies while European allies' access is limited — cited as a European security concern ([[2026-08-05 Europe must do more to harness its AI ambitions]]).
- **2026-08-03** — Target of thinly-veiled criticism from [[Palantir]] CEO Alex Karp ("tiny group of people" moralizing about AI use). Amodei's defence-use limits have led to a protracted legal dispute with the Pentagon ([[2026-08-03 Palantir forecasts greater US demand for AI software]]).
- **2026-08-02** — Mythos was used by researchers at Calif to defeat Apple's Memory Integrity Enforcement — the first memory-corruption exploit on that software; Apple also credits Anthropic tools in its own security fixes ([[2026-08-02 Apple struggles with AI bug hunters]]).
