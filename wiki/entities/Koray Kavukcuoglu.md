# Koray Kavukcuoglu

Former chief technology officer of [[Google DeepMind]]; since August 2026 he holds day-to-day control of the lab as **senior vice-president of Google DeepMind** — pointedly not chief executive, the title [[Demis Hassabis]] held ([[2026-08-05 Google DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis steps aside]], [[2026-08-06 Google seeks a sharper focus in AI after Hassabis move]]).

## Key facts

- **Relocated from London to Google's Mountain View headquarters in 2025**, and leads development of Gemini **from a desk beside [[Sergey Brin]]** ([[2026-08-06 Google's AI shake-up boosts Brin as Hassabis steps aside]]).
- Hassabis had been handing him operational responsibility for Gemini development over several months before the announcement ([[2026-08-06 Google's AI shake-up boosts Brin as Hassabis steps aside]]).
- The SVP title is read as implying **less autonomy for the lab and closer alignment with other Google divisions** than under a CEO ([[2026-08-06 Google seeks a sharper focus in AI after Hassabis move]]).

## Why it matters

His appointment is the mechanism of [[Google's AI reorganisation]]: geography (London → Mountain View), title (CEO → SVP), and proximity (beside Brin) all point the same direction. The brief he inherits is specific — Google's executives and board are worried about **coding agents and enterprise AI**, where [[OpenAI]] and [[Anthropic]] lead ([[2026-08-06 Google seeks a sharper focus in AI after Hassabis move]]).
