# Situational Awareness

AI-focused hedge fund founded 2024 by **Leopold Aschenbrenner** (OpenAI alumnus, author of the influential 2024 "Situational Awareness" essays). The poster child for the gap between being right about AI and surviving the trade.

## Recent developments

- **2026-08-01** — After banks called loans on its leveraged AI-stock positions during the market rout, the fund sold most public holdings to **Citadel**. Average share price of its 29 end-of-March US holdings: -21% in a month; Sandisk -45%. Aschenbrenner claims still +80% on the year; retains unleveraged stocks and an [[Anthropic]] stake ([[2026-08-01 Situational Awareness got the future right]]).

- **2026-08-17** — The loss now has a headline number: FT Alphaville's all-time trading-loss leaderboard was compiled "following Leopold Aschenbrenner's **$35bn** trading hit last month" ([[2026-08-17 FirstFT Trump's eleventh-hour demand to reduce drills]]). Squares awkwardly with his claim of being +80% on the year — previously reported without a loss figure per [[2026-08-01 Situational Awareness got the future right]]; not superseded, but the two framings sit side by side.

- **2026-08-19** — The contagion ledger: **Jane Street reported a $15bn July loss** partly from an investment in Situational Awareness (plus other AI stocks) — the fund's collapse burned its own investors, though Jane Street still made $40bn+ in net trading revenue on the year ([[2026-08-19 Hudson River posts $11.4bn trading windfall]]).
