# Apple

iPhone maker, navigating the AI era on two fronts: a trade-secrets war with [[OpenAI]] over talent and hardware designs, and an AI-driven flood of security bug reports overwhelming its processes.

## Recent developments

- **2026-08-06** — OpenAI moved to dismiss, alleging Apple's real aim is stopping employee departures and that Apple's own practices caused the disputed data handling: it authorised Chang Liu's file retrieval, and encouraged staff to use **personal iCloud accounts for work**, "intermingling company and personal data." Hearing set for October ([[2026-08-06 OpenAI says Apple lawsuit aims to stop employees leaving]]).
- **2026-08-04** — Sought a preliminary injunction against OpenAI and former employees, plus expedited discovery; OpenAI publicly rebutted the suit the same day. See [[OpenAI–Apple trade secrets fight]] ([[2026-08-04 OpenAI hits back at Apple trade secrets fight]]).
- **2026-08-02** — Capped researcher bug-report submissions (June) under a deluge of AI-generated reports; an Italian startup found 50+ macOS bugs with ChatGPT including a privilege-escalation chain it initially couldn't report. This week's security updates carried ~5× the usual fixes, with credits to OpenAI and Anthropic tools ([[2026-08-02 Apple struggles with AI bug hunters]]).

## Background

- Partnered with Google to revamp Siri after its earlier OpenAI integration soured ([[2026-08-04 OpenAI hits back at Apple trade secrets fight]]).
- Its Memory Integrity Enforcement (Sept 2025) was billed as the biggest consumer-OS memory-safety upgrade ever; defeated within 8 months via Anthropic's Mythos ([[2026-08-02 Apple struggles with AI bug hunters]]).
