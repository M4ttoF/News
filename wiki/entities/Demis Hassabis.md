# Demis Hassabis

Founder of DeepMind (2010), Nobel laureate (Chemistry, for AlphaFold), knighted. Ran [[Google DeepMind]] as CEO until August 2026.

## Recent developments

- **2026-08-06** — Partial answer to the elevation-or-sidelining question: **both readings have named sources.** People familiar with Google's thinking cite executive frustration with his lesser commercial focus, and internal tension over releasing AlphaFold freely for little commercial return. A person close to the company disputes any tension. A friend calls it a good outcome — he keeps influence **without relocating to the US** and gains time for **Isomorphic Labs**, the drug-discovery company he founded inside Alphabet in 2021. His own framing: focusing on the "big picture" and the direction of AGI, which he says **"is close at hand"** ([[2026-08-06 Google's AI shake-up boosts Brin as Hassabis steps aside]], [[2026-08-06 Google seeks a sharper focus in AI after Hassabis move]]). See [[Google's AI reorganisation]].
- **2026-08-05** — Stepped down as Google DeepMind CEO; becomes DeepMind **chair** and **chief scientist of Alphabet**. Succeeded operationally by [[Koray Kavukcuoglu]] ([[2026-08-05 Google DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis steps aside]]).

## Background

- Sold DeepMind to Google for **£400mn in 2014**; Nobel Prize in Chemistry for AlphaFold in **2024**; knighted.
- Named alongside Schmidhuber and Hinton as European researchers who pioneered modern AI that US and Chinese companies then commercialised ([[2026-08-06 Europe must create an AI money-mobilisation machine]]).
- Now one of the industry's leading public voices on AI, and increasingly focused on [[Agentic AI and automated science]] via Isomorphic Labs.
