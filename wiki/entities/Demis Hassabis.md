# Demis Hassabis

Founder of DeepMind (2010), Nobel laureate (Chemistry, for AlphaFold), knighted. Ran [[Google DeepMind]] as CEO until August 2026.

## Recent developments

- **2026-08-05** — Stepped down as Google DeepMind CEO; becomes DeepMind **chair** and **chief scientist of Alphabet**. Succeeded operationally by Koray Kavukcuoglu ([[2026-08-05 Google DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis steps aside]]). Open question worth watching: whether this is elevation (Alphabet-wide science remit) or sidelining as Google consolidates AI around Gemini product divisions.
