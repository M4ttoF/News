# Amazon

Cloud (AWS) and ecommerce giant executing a both-horses AI strategy: large equity positions in **both** leading labs, leveraged to push its own silicon and cloud.

## Recent developments

- **2026-07-31** — Completed the full $50bn investment in [[OpenAI]] (~5% stake, $852bn valuation), releasing the conditional $35bn tranche despite milestones (IPO or AI breakthrough) not being met ([[2026-07-31 Amazon completes 50bn investment in OpenAI]]).

## Background

- Up to $33bn committed to [[Anthropic]]; $18bn invested to date ([[2026-07-31 Amazon completes 50bn investment in OpenAI]]).
- Uses both labs to drive adoption of its **Trainium** chips (competing with Nvidia and Google's TPU) and to guarantee frontier models on AWS ([[2026-07-31 Amazon completes 50bn investment in OpenAI]]).
