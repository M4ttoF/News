# Nvidia

The dominant supplier of AI training and inference silicon, led by **Jensen Huang**. As of August 2026 it is also becoming a financial intermediary for the AI build-out — orchestrating the debt that funds purchases of its own chips ([[2026-08-11 Nvidia becomes the bank of AI]]).

## Recent developments

- **2026-08-11** — Formed a financing partnership with **Apollo, Blackstone, BlackRock, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs and KKR** to raise **over $500bn** in "compute financing platforms" for frontier labs, enterprises and AI clouds — described as arguably the most ambitious financing plan ever to hit Wall Street. Huang said Nvidia may guarantee **up to 25% of project residual value**. Still subject to final agreement ([[2026-08-11 Nvidia becomes the bank of AI]]). See [[AI infrastructure financing]].

## Background

- The company startups are trying to unseat: a 2026 funding wave backs specialized inference silicon pitched as cheaper than Nvidia's general-purpose GPUs (Olix, Etched, SambaNova, Fractile) ([[2026-08-03 Olix triples valuation to 3.3bn]]). See [[Nvidia challengers - the AI chip startup wave]].
- Hyperscalers are also building in-house alternatives — [[Amazon]]'s Trainium ([[2026-07-31 Amazon completes 50bn investment in OpenAI]]).
- Olix's differentiator (photonic interconnect) is designed partly to sidestep the scarce HBM memory and advanced packaging capacity that constrains Nvidia's supply chain ([[2026-08-03 Olix triples valuation to 3.3bn]]).
